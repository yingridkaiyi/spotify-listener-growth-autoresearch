## Current Project Direction

Instead of predicting concert sellout, the project now predicts a 30-day listener
growth target that can be built directly from available historical exports.

That makes the Week 1 scope more realistic because:

- the target is directly observable
- the unit of analysis is simpler to scale
- the split can be frozen by time
- the evaluation loop is easier to control
