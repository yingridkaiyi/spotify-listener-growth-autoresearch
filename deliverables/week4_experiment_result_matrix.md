Generalization-focused conclusion:

- The earlier 58/42 ensemble remains the lowest validation-RMSE run in the
  full Week 4 search history.
- The new conservative ensembles confirmed that nonlinear tree corrections can
  reduce validation RMSE substantially.
- The best conservative ensemble,
  `search_week4_ratio_family_blend_huber125_extra400l6_w65_v1`, still posted
  worse final test RMSE `2,019,078.49` and worse test Spearman `0.322881`
  than the ratio-family Huber anchor.
- Under the updated Week 4 guardrails, the simpler ratio-family Huber model is
  retained because it generalizes better even though it is not the historical
  best validation-only run.
